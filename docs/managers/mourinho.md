# José Mourinho: Defensive Organisation and Counter-Attacking

## Summary
José Mourinho is a Portuguese football manager. He has managed clubs in several countries, including Porto, Chelsea, Inter and Real Madrid. His teams are known for organised defending, tactical discipline and fast counter-attacks. He won league titles with Porto, Chelsea, Inter and Real Madrid, and the Champions League with Porto in 2004 and Inter in 2010.

## What is his philosophy?
Mourinho builds his teams from the defence forward. The team stays compact, keeps short distances between the lines and gives the opponent little space in central areas. Players are expected to know their jobs and keep their positions. When the team wins the ball, it attacks quickly into the space the opponent has left. He plans each match around the opponent, so his teams change their approach from game to game.

## How did his Chelsea teams play?
Mourinho managed Chelsea from 2004 to 2007 and again from 2013 to 2015. In his first spell, the team won the Premier League in 2004-05 and 2005-06. It played a 4-3-3 with Claude Makélélé as the holding midfielder in front of the defence, John Terry in central defence, Petr Čech in goal and Didier Drogba as the striker. In his second spell, Chelsea won the Premier League again in 2014-15.

## How did his Inter team play?
Mourinho managed Inter from 2008 to 2010. In 2009-10, Inter won Serie A, the Coppa Italia and the Champions League. The team often played a 4-2-3-1, with Wesley Sneijder behind a striker such as Diego Milito. Inter defended deep and compactly in its Champions League semi-final against Barcelona, and then won the final against Bayern Munich.

## How did his Real Madrid team play?
Mourinho managed Real Madrid from 2010 to 2013. The team usually played a 4-2-3-1 and was known for fast counter-attacks, with Cristiano Ronaldo, Ángel Di María and Karim Benzema in attack. Real Madrid won the Copa del Rey in 2010-11 and La Liga in 2011-12, with a record 100 points in the league.

## What are the main criticisms?
Some critics say his teams can be too cautious, especially in big matches, and that he relies on a deep defence and counters. Others say his teams can struggle when the opponent sits back and leaves no space to attack. Supporters point to his trophies and to how well his teams are organised.
