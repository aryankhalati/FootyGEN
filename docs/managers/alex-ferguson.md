# Sir Alex Ferguson: The Treble Side and the 2007-2009 Team

## Summary
Sir Alex Ferguson is a Scottish football manager. He managed Manchester United from 1986 to 2013 and won 13 Premier League titles with the club. His teams were known for fast, attacking football, strong wide play and a refusal to give up in matches. Two of his best-known sides are the treble winners of 1998-99 and the team of 2007 to 2009 with Cristiano Ronaldo, Carlos Tevez and Wayne Rooney.

## What was his philosophy?
Ferguson built his teams around attacking play, wide players and quick passing. He changed his team often and adapted his shape to the players he had and the opponent in front of him. He used a 4-4-2 for much of his time at the club, and in later years often a 4-3-3 or a similar shape with three forwards. He also gave young players a chance and rebuilt his squads over time.

## How did the treble side play?
In 1998-99, United won the Premier League, the FA Cup and the Champions League in the same season. The team played with wingers, with David Beckham on the right and Ryan Giggs on the left, and Roy Keane and Paul Scholes in the centre of midfield. Dwight Yorke and Andy Cole led the attack, with Teddy Sheringham and Ole Gunnar Solskjaer as options from the bench. Peter Schmeichel played in goal.

## How did United win the Champions League in 1999?
United played Bayern Munich in the final in Barcelona. Bayern led for most of the match, but United scored twice in stoppage time. Sheringham scored the first goal and Solskjaer the second, and United won 2-1.

## How did the 2007-2009 team play?
Between 2006-07 and 2008-09, United won three Premier League titles in a row. The team had fast, mobile forwards in Ronaldo, Tevez and Rooney, who often swapped positions to confuse defenders. Nemanja Vidić and Rio Ferdinand defended in the centre, Edwin van der Sar played in goal, and Michael Carrick and Scholes controlled midfield.

## What did the 2007-2009 team win in Europe?
United won the Champions League in 2007-08, beating Chelsea on penalties in the final in Moscow. In 2008-09, United reached the final again, but lost to Barcelona in Rome.

## What are the main criticisms?
Some critics point to United's weaker European record in some seasons, and to the cost of replacing key players. Supporters point to the number of trophies and to the way he rebuilt his teams again and again over many years.
