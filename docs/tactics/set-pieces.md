# Attacking and Defending Set Pieces

## Summary
A set piece is a way of restarting play when the ball is dead, such as a corner, a free kick or a throw-in. The attacking team can plan its positions and movements in advance, and the defending team must organise itself to stop them. Set pieces are a part of the match that teams can practise on the training pitch, and they can decide close games.

## What are the main types of set piece?
The main types are corners, free kicks, throw-ins and penalties. A direct free kick can be scored from without another player touching the ball, and an indirect free kick cannot. A goal cannot be scored directly from a throw-in. A player cannot be offside from a corner, a throw-in or a goal kick. Teams plan differently for each type, depending on where the ball is and how far it is from goal.

## How do teams attack set pieces?
The kicker aims the ball at a chosen area, often with an inswinging or outswinging delivery. Attackers make runs from different positions, to confuse the markers and make space for a header or a shot. Some players block defenders, and others act as decoys. A team can also play a short corner or a short free kick, to change the angle of the delivery and pull defenders out of position.

## How do teams defend set pieces?
There are two main systems. In zonal marking, each defender protects an area and attacks any ball that enters it. In man-marking, each defender follows one attacker. Many teams combine the two, with some players in zones and others marking one on one. Defenders also decide who stays near the edge of the area, to deal with a ball that is cleared short, and who is ready for a counter-attack.

## What does a team need to do well?
The attacking team needs a player who can deliver the ball accurately and players who are strong in the air. The defending team needs good communication, so that every player knows their job. Goalkeepers also matter, because they decide when to come for the ball and when to stay on the line. Practice helps all of this, because the movements are planned in advance.

## What are the risks?
A poor delivery wastes the chance and can give the opponent the ball for a counter-attack. For the defending team, one player who loses their marker or misjudges a zone can concede a goal. A team that sends many players forward on a set piece leaves fewer players to defend if the ball is cleared.

## Which teams are known for it?
Arsenal under Mikel Arteta became one of the best teams at set pieces in recent years. They won the Premier League in 2025-26 and reached the Champions League final that season, where they lost to Paris Saint-Germain on penalties.
