# Building from the Back

## Summary
Building from the back is a way of starting an attack in which the team passes the ball from its own goalkeeper and defenders, instead of kicking it long. The aim is to move the ball up the pitch in a controlled way and keep possession. It is a style of play, not a formation, and teams can use it in a 4-3-3 or in other shapes.

## What is building from the back?
The first phase of an attack happens in the team's own half. The goalkeeper and centre-backs pass the ball between them, and then play it to midfielders or full-backs. This is often called the build-up phase. The team uses short passes to draw the opponent forward, and then looks for a pass into the space that opens up.

## How does the team set up?
The centre-backs usually spread wide, to give each other room and make the pitch bigger for the opponent to cover. The goalkeeper acts as an extra passing option. A No. 6 often drops close to the defenders to receive the ball, and the full-backs may move forward or into midfield. The team tries to have players in different lines, so there is always a pass available.

## What does a team need to do this well?
Defenders and the goalkeeper must be comfortable receiving the ball under pressure and passing accurately with both feet. Players also need good awareness, so they can see where the opponent is pressing before they receive the ball. Movement matters too, because a player who stands still is easy to mark.

## How does the opponent respond?
Opponents often use a high press to take away the passing options and win the ball near the goal. Some mark the players one against one, and some close down the passing lanes. If the team can play past the press, it has open space in front of it. If not, it can play a longer pass to avoid losing the ball.

## What are the risks?
A lost ball close to the team's own goal gives the opponent a good chance to score. A poor pass from the goalkeeper or a centre-back is hard to recover from. The style needs confidence and practice, and it is harder against a team that presses well.

## Which teams are known for it?
Pep Guardiola's teams at Barcelona, Bayern Munich and Manchester City are well known for building from the back, with the goalkeeper involved in the passing.
