# False 9 Role

## Summary
A false 9 is a central forward who starts in the striker's position but repeatedly drops into midfield. The role changes the shape of the attack. The team has an extra player in midfield, and the defenders must decide whether to follow. It is often seen in a 4-3-3. A striker who only occasionally drops deep is not necessarily a false 9.

## What does a false 9 do?
A false 9 starts as the central forward, then moves back into the space between the opposition's midfield and defence. From there the player receives the ball, links play and passes to runners. The false 9 can also dribble or shoot. The key feature is that the dropping movement is repeated and planned. It is part of the team's attack, not a one-off.

## Why is a false 9 hard to defend?
The false 9 creates a dilemma for the defence. If a centre-back follows the false 9 into midfield, space opens behind him. If the centre-back stays in the defensive line, the false 9 receives the ball between the lines and the team has an extra midfielder. Either choice gives the attack something. Defenders must communicate to decide who follows and who stays.

## How is a false 9 different from a traditional No. 9?
A traditional No. 9 occupies the centre-backs, runs in behind and gives the team a target for crosses. A false 9 does the opposite and moves away from the defence to find the ball. A false 9 is also different from a No. 10. A No. 10 starts behind the striker, while a false 9 starts as the striker and then drops.

## What do teammates need to do?
The false 9 only works if teammates attack the space left behind. The wide forwards and the midfield runners need to move into the area that the false 9 leaves. The No. 8s can make late runs into the box. Teammates must also adjust their positions to keep width and balance. If nobody attacks the vacated space, the defenders can stay deep and the false 9 has little to exploit.

## What are the risks of using a false 9?
A false 9 who drops deep leaves no forward to occupy the centre-backs unless others take that job. The team can also struggle if the wingers are isolated or if teammates do not adjust their positions to keep balance. A team with a false 9 needs players who can run in behind, because the false 9 mainly creates space for others.

## Who is an example of a false 9?
Lionel Messi played as a false 9 for Pep Guardiola's Barcelona, increasingly from 2009. He dropped into midfield and let the wide forwards and midfield runners attack the space he left. Barcelona used this system for patient possession, short passing and positional rotations.

## What qualities does a false 9 need?
The role needs a good first touch and close control, because the player receives the ball with defenders around them. Vision and passing to find runners, plus dribbling to beat a defender when the space opens, matter most. A false 9 also needs intelligent movement to choose when to drop and when to stay high, and finishing ability, since the role still has to score. Scanning before receiving helps the player turn and play the team forward.
